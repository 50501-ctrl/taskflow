Built for focus

Everything you need to plan, track and review your work in one place.

  • Kanban boards

    Drag tasks between To do, In progress and Done. Custom columns and swimlanes included.

  • AI analytics

    Weekly insights show overloaded days and suggest a healthier schedule.

  • Smart reminders

    Notifications arrive when you can act on them, not in the middle of deep work.

  • Team sync

    Share boards, assign owners and see changes in real time on every device.

  • Offline mode

    Keep working without a connection. Everything syncs when you are back online.

  • Burnout meter

    A simple daily score tells you when to slow down and when to push.
